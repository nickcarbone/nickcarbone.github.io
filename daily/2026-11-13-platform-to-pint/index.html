Most brewery visits involve a car, which defeats the point. This guide is for the other kind: twenty breweries in seven countries where you can step off a train, walk for a quarter of an hour at most, and be drinking beer brewed on the premises.

They range from a Brussels lambic brewery that brewed its first beer in 1938 to a Copenhagen brewpub that opened in 2015. Two are in railway buildings: one in the portico of a Leipzig terminus from 1842, the other in an arch of the Berlin viaduct. One Bamberg street has two breweries facing each other. Batemans, the furthest from a city, is four minutes from a halt on a Lincolnshire branch line.

How the walks were measured. Brewery and station positions come from OpenStreetMap.1 Each walk was routed on foot from the station building or platform entrance to the brewery door with the OSRM walking profile, which assumes a steady pace of about 4.5 km/h.2 Where a brewery publishes its own walking time, it is quoted alongside. The streets listed under each entry are the named streets on that route, in order; unnamed paths and station forecourts are left out. Each entry’s map is drawn from that same routed path, and its links open the brewery’s own website and the walk on OpenStreetMap.

What qualifies. Beer has to be brewed at the address you walk to, even if most of the brewery’s output is now made elsewhere, as at Carlsberg and ’t IJ. That rule kept out some famous names. De Koninck in Antwerp, for instance, moved production of its Bolleke to Duvel Moortgat’s Breendonk brewery in 2025, though the Antwerp brewery’s café and tours carry on. Opening hours, prices and train times change too often for an evergreen guide and are left out; check them before you go.

BE

Brasserie Cantillon

Brussels, Belgium · Rue Gheude 56, Anderlecht

The one survivor of the hundred-plus breweries Brussels had in 1900.

Bruxelles-Midi / Brussel-Zuid10 min · 776 m

Via Place Bara → Rue Gheude2

A junction of several main lines, including line 50A to Ghent, Bruges and Ostend.6

Official websiteWalking route on OpenStreetMap

When Paul Cantillon and Marie Troch set up in Cureghem in 1900, they were gueuze blenders, buying young lambic from other brewers and blending it. They did not brew a drop of their own until 1938, after buying the equipment of a brewery in Ouffet in 1937 and moving it to Rue Gheude.3 Brussels then had more than a hundred breweries; Cantillon was the only one still working into the 2000s.4

Everything here is spontaneously fermented: the wort cools in open coolships and wild yeasts from the air start the fermentation. The brewery went organic in 1999 and makes only lambic and its blends.4 Jean-Pierre Van Roy, who took over in 1969, opened the working brewery to visitors as the Brussels Gueuze Museum in 1978.35

On tap34

  • Gueuze blended lambic
  • Kriek cherry lambic
  • Grand Cru Bruocsella lambic

At the brewery

Self-guided visits with printed guides in French, Dutch and English; guided group tours by reservation.5 There is a shop on site.3

BE

Brouwerij De Halve Maan

Bruges, Belgium · Walplein 26

A family brewery in the old town that pipes its beer out under the streets.

Brugge14 min · 1007 m

Via Stationsplein → Walplein2

On the Brussels–Ghent–Bruges–Ostend main line (50A), with branches to Blankenberge and Kortrijk.9

Official websiteWalking route on OpenStreetMap

The brewery says there has been brewing on this site for about five centuries; its own family story starts in 1856, when Leon Maes took over.78 Six generations on, the beer is still brewed here, in the middle of a city of narrow cobbled lanes, which created an unusual problem: getting it to the bottling plant on the edge of town without a procession of tankers.

The answer, completed in 2016, was a pipeline. It runs for about 3.3 km (Wikipedia gives 3,276 m) from the brewhouse to the bottling hall, reaching 34 m below ground at its deepest, and was partly paid for by crowdfunding, with backers rewarded in beer.78 Brugse Zot, the house blond launched in 2005, is the flagship; Straffe Hendrik dates from 1981.7

On tap7

  • Brugse Zot top-fermented blond
  • Straffe Hendrik strong ales, as Tripel and Quadrupel
  • Sportzot non-alcoholic, launched 2018

At the brewery

Restaurant and bar on site; guided tours run daily and end with a glass of Brugse Zot.10

BE

Gentse Stadsbrouwerij Gruut

Ghent, Belgium · Rembert Dodoensdreef 31a

A city brewery that flavours its beer the way Flanders did before hops.

Gent-Dampoort13 min · 936 m

Via Schoolkaai → Napoleon de Pauwbrug → Hagelandkaai → Sint-Jorisbrug → Steendam → Rembert Dodoensdreef2

On line 58 towards Eeklo and line 59 towards Antwerp via Sint-Niklaas. Most intercity trains call at Gent-Sint-Pieters instead, which is 3.5 km from Gruut.132

Official websiteWalking route on OpenStreetMap

Before hops took over, Low Countries brewers seasoned beer with gruut, a blend of herbs. Gruut, which opened in 2009, makes beer on that principle, using a herb mixture in place of hops; the concept was Annick De Splenter’s, whose family had brewed for generations.11

The brewing vats are on view, and the brewery runs tastings, including a “beer alchemist” session in which you adjust a Gruut Blond to your own taste.12

The brewery’s most recent material found for this guide dates from 2023; check it is open before travelling.

On tap11

  • Gruut Blond unfiltered blond, 5.5%
  • Gruut Wit herbal wheat beer, 5%
  • Gruut Amber 5.9%
  • Gruut Inferno strong golden ale, 8%

At the brewery

Bar with tastings and group sessions.12

NL

Brouwerij 't IJ

Amsterdam, Netherlands · Funenkade 7

Amsterdam’s best-known small brewery, in a former bathhouse under a windmill.

Amsterdam Muiderpoort13 min · 980 m

Via Dapperstraat → Zeeburgerpad2

East of Amsterdam Centraal, where the lines to Utrecht and Amersfoort part.16

Official websiteWalking route on OpenStreetMap

Kaspar Peterson, a musician, started brewing in October 1985 in a disused municipal bathhouse beside De Gooyer windmill. The building’s water supply, drains, tiles and steam plant suited a brewery.1415

Demand outgrew the bathhouse, and in 2013 a second brewery opened about 700 m away on Zeeburgerpad; since 2015 the company has worked with Belgium’s Duvel Moortgat, now its parent.1415 The original site still brews for the tasting room, and the label still carries the ostrich and egg.1415

On tap15

  • Natte
  • Zatte
  • Columbus
  • Struis

At the brewery

Proeflokaal de Molen serves snacks rather than meals: peanuts, boiled eggs, sausage and cheese. Brewery tours are listed on its site.17

NL

Poesiat & Kater

Amsterdam, Netherlands · Polderweg 648

A revival of what was once the largest brewery in the Netherlands.

Amsterdam Muiderpoort7 min · 524 m

Via Polderweg → Oranje-Vrijstaatkade2

The same station as ’t IJ, so two breweries share one stop.16

Official websiteWalking route on OpenStreetMap

In 1733 Jan van den Bosch opened a brewery and vinegar works called De Gekroonde Valk on the Hoogte Kadijk. Renamed after Jan Messchert van Vollenhoven, who took it over in 1791, it was the largest brewery in the Netherlands around 1900, best known for Van Vollenhoven’s Extra Stout.18 Heineken bought it and closed the brewery in the 1940s, though it kept making the stout for decades before dropping it.19

A foundation holding the original recipe brought the stout back in 2006, and in 2014 Jesse van Vollenhoven asked it to start a brewery again.18 Named after two 19th-century Gekroonde Valk brewery workers, Poesiat & Kater opened in March 2017 in an 1887 building on the old Oostergasfabriek site.1819 You can watch the brewing from the bar, and the beer goes straight into the bar’s serving tanks.18

On tap18

  • Van Vollenhoven’s Extra Stout the revived 19th-century stout
  • House beers six to nine on tap, rotating

At the brewery

Meals inside and on the terrace, lunch and evening.18

Hamburg Sternschanze6 min · 473 m

Via station forecourt and footpaths2

An S-Bahn station; the Schanzenhöfe sit right next to it.21

Official websiteWalking route on OpenStreetMap

Ratsherrn has brewed in the heritage-listed Schanzenhöfe since 2012, and in 2020 became the first north German member of the Slow Brewing association.20 The restored buildings now hold restaurants, cafés and studios as well as the brewery.21

The brewery’s restaurant, Altes Mädchen, serves organic food and more than 60 beers, and the brewery shop stocks about 300 from around the world.21 Tours take in about a thousand years of Hamburg brewing history before the tasting.22

DE

Brauerei Schumacher

Düsseldorf, Germany · Oststraße 123

The oldest of Düsseldorf’s Altbier house breweries, still brewing behind its own bar.

Düsseldorf Hauptbahnhof11 min · 797 m

Via Konrad-Adenauer-Platz → Bismarckstraße → Oststraße2

The city’s main station; the walk is a straight run up Bismarckstraße.2

Official websiteWalking route on OpenStreetMap

In 1838 Johann Matthias Schumacher bought a brewery called Im Sonnenaufgang in the old town; Düsseldorf’s tourist board calls the business the city’s oldest house brewery.2324 It has long since moved to Oststraße, and every litre of Schumacher Alt is brewed, fermented, stored and filtered there.24

Schumacher Alt is top-fermented with the brewery’s own yeast.23 Matthias Schumacher is also credited with Latzenbier, a stronger, hoppier Alt that goes on sale only three times a year. Its name comes from “op de Latt”, the top cellar shelf where he kept the first barrel.23

On tap23

  • Schumacher Alt amber Altbier, 4.6%
  • Latzen stronger Alt, 5.5%, three times a year
  • 1838er anniversary beer first brewed in 2013

At the brewery

Classic brewery food and Rhineland dishes, made in-house.25

DE

Das Lemke

Berlin, Germany · Dircksenstraße, S-Bahnbogen 143

A brewery built inside the arches of the railway viaduct itself.

Berlin Hackescher Markt2 min · 188 m

Via Am Zwirngraben2

On the elevated Stadtbahn, served by S3, S5, S7 and S9.28

Official websiteWalking route on OpenStreetMap

In 1999 Oliver Lemke and friends converted a run-down arch of the railway viaduct at Hackescher Markt into a brewery, which opened on 21 November 1999.26 Its first beer, a dark lager called the Original, was brewed here.27

The company’s main brewhouse, installed in 2018, sits further along the same viaduct towards Alexanderplatz. The original arch became Lemke’s pilot brewery, so small batches are still brewed on site.26 The bar and restaurant in the arch are now called Das Lemke.27

On tap27

  • Lemke Original dark lager, the first beer brewed here

At the brewery

Berlin and German dishes made by hand, and a courtyard beer garden.2729

DE

Gosebrauerei Bayerischer Bahnhof

Leipzig, Germany · Bayrischer Platz 1

A brewery in the portico of a railway terminus opened in 1842.

Leipzig Bayerischer Bahnhof3 min · 216 m

Via station forecourt and footpaths2

A City-Tunnel S-Bahn station, opened in December 2013 beneath the old terminus.30

Official websiteWalking route on OpenStreetMap

The Bavarian Station opened in 1842 as the Leipzig terminus of the line to Hof and Bavaria; Wikipedia calls it Germany’s oldest preserved railway station, and the brewery goes further and bills it as the oldest preserved terminus in the world.3031 Long-distance trains stopped using it in 1912, when the new main station opened, and it was badly damaged in the Second World War.32

Rebuilding began in 1999 and the brewery-restaurant served its first guests on 19 July 2000.32 Since December 2013 S-Bahn trains have stopped directly beneath it.30 The speciality is Gose, a top-fermented, salty and sour wheat beer. The house says it has been brewed in Leipzig since 1738, and the railway theme runs into the beer names: Schaffner (conductor) and Heizer (stoker).31

On tap3133

  • Original Leipziger Gose top-fermented, salty-sour wheat beer
  • Schaffner cloudy Pils
  • Kuppler wheat beer
  • Heizer strong dark beer, Schwarzbier

At the brewery

Seasonal regional cooking and bread baked in-house.3133

Brauerei Spezial

Bamberg, Germany · Obere Königstraße 10

Smoked beer from a brewery that still kilns its own malt over beech.

Bamberg10 min · 750 m

Via Ludwigstraße → Luitpoldstraße → Obere Königstraße2

Regional hub on the lines to Nuremberg, Würzburg and Hof, and the northern end of Nuremberg S-Bahn line S1.38

Official websiteWalking route on OpenStreetMap

Spezial was first recorded in 1536 and has belonged to the Merz family since 1898.3435 Bamberg’s tourist office counts it as one of only two breweries in the city keeping the traditional smoked-beer craft going. Spezial still makes its own smoked malt, soaking, germinating and then kilning the barley over an open beechwood fire on the premises.3635

The smoke is gentler than at Schlenkerla, the city’s other famous smoked-beer brewery.37 The association of brewery inns lists Spezial as about five minutes on foot from the station; the routed walk is closer to ten.352

On tap3435

  • Rauchbier Lager smoked amber lager
  • Märzen smoked, darker and maltier
  • Ungespundet unsmoked, unfiltered, at the inn only

At the brewery

Franconian home cooking, including vegetarian dishes, and guest rooms upstairs.35

Augustiner Bräustuben

Munich, Germany · Landsberger Straße 19

Munich’s oldest brewery, with its tavern in the old stables next to the brewhouse.

München Hackerbrücke6 min · 445 m

Via Landsberger Straße2

On the S-Bahn trunk line through the city centre, served by every S-Bahn line.45

Official websiteWalking route on OpenStreetMap

Augustiner was first mentioned in 1328, which makes it Munich’s oldest surviving brewery. It moved out of the old town to Landsberger Straße in the 1880s and brewed there for the first time in 1884.4142 Since 1996 just over half of it has belonged to a charitable foundation set up by Edith Haberland-Wagner of the owning family.43

The Bräustuben opened in 1994 in the brewery’s former stables, where the horses that pull the Oktoberfest beer wagons were kept.44 Since 1987 Augustiner has been the only Munich brewery at the Oktoberfest to serve exclusively from wooden barrels.41

On tap43

  • Lagerbier Hell pale lager
  • Edelstoff stronger pale lager
  • Dunkel dark lager
  • Weißbier wheat beer

At the brewery

Bavarian food: roast pork, knuckle, cold platters and vegetarian dishes.44

Augustiner Bräu Kloster Mülln

Salzburg, Austria · Lindhofstraße 7

A monastery brewery from 1621 that still pours from wooden casks into stone mugs.

Salzburg Mülln-Altstadt6 min · 478 m

Via Müllner Hauptstraße2

An S-Bahn halt opened in December 2009; the brewery’s own directions point to it.4849

Official websiteWalking route on OpenStreetMap

Augustinian monks founded the brewery in 1621. It calls itself Austria’s biggest beer tavern, with about 2,400 seats across its halls and a chestnut-shaded garden.4647 Benedictines from Michaelbeuern Abbey took it over in 1835, and the brewhouse they built in 1912 still stands in its original form.47

Beer is still poured from wooden casks into stoneware mugs.4647 One of the halls, the Abt-Nicolaus-Saal added in 2017, is the former marble hall of Salzburg’s main railway station.47

On tap47

  • Märzen the year-round house beer
  • Fastenbier Lent
  • Bockbier late autumn and Advent

At the brewery

Bring your own food, or buy it from the stalls in the hall’s corridor of food stands. Drinks must be bought from the house. Guided brewery tours for booked groups.4750

Pilsner Urquell Brewery

Plzeň, Czech Republic · U Prazdroje 7

The birthplace of pale lager, across a footbridge from the station.

Plzeň hlavní nádraží8 min · 639 m

Via station forecourt and footpaths2

Hub for line 170 (Prague–Cheb) and line 180 to Furth im Wald on the German border, among others.54

Official websiteWalking route on OpenStreetMap

The citizens of Plzeň founded their Burghers’ Brewery in 1839 and hired a Bavarian brewer, Josef Groll, who brewed the first batch of the golden lager on 5 October 1842.5152 The name Pilsner Urquell (“from the original source”) was trademarked in 1898.51

The brewery is now owned by Asahi.51 Tours end in the historic cellars with unfiltered, unpasteurised lager drawn from oak barrels.52 The brewery’s directions say the walk takes about five minutes: head for Nádražní street and take the footbridge into the brewery grounds.53

  • Unfiltered cellar beer from oak, on the tour

St Austell Brewery

St Austell, England · 63 Trevarthian Road

The Cornish brewer that turned an eclipse beer into its flagship.

St Austell8 min · 590 m

Via Trevarthian Road2

On the Cornish Main Line between Plymouth and Penzance.68

Official websiteWalking route on OpenStreetMap

Walter Hicks founded the brewery in 1851 and first brewed at the Seven Stars Inn. The Trevarthian Road brewhouse went up in 1893, and the beer is still made there in a system fed by gravity rather than pumps.66 After Hicks died in 1916 his daughter, Hester Parnall, took charge and helped keep the company independent.67

Tribute, now its best-known beer, began as a one-off called Daylight Robbery for the solar eclipse of 1999.66 The brewery marked 175 years in 2026 with a history by its archivist, Paul Holden.67

DK

Home of Carlsberg

Copenhagen, Denmark · Gamle Carlsberg Vej 11

The founding site of Carlsberg, where a small house brewery still works.

Carlsberg7 min · 555 m

Via Tapperitorvet2

On the S-tog line to Høje Taastrup; opened in 2016 to replace Enghave station, 200 m to the east.81

Official websiteWalking route on OpenStreetMap

J.C. Jacobsen built his first brewery on Valby Bakke in 1847. Most production moved to Fredericia in 2008, but beer is still brewed at the top of the hill by Husbryggeriet Jacobsen, the site’s house brewery.77 The house beers are served straight from its cellar tanks and are available only here.78

Two discoveries made at the Carlsberg Laboratory reached far beyond beer. In 1883 Emil Christian Hansen worked out how to cultivate pure yeast, and in 1909 Søren Sørensen devised the pH scale.79 After a renovation the visitor centre reopened as Home of Carlsberg on 1 December 2023, with its collection of more than 22,000 unopened bottles and the brewery’s draught horses back in the stables.80
