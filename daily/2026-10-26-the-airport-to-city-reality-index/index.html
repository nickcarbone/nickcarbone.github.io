The Airport-to-City Reality Index

Thirty European airports scored on how fast, how often and how late public transport gets you from the terminal to the middle of town, with the scoring rules on show.

Published 26 October 2026 · Timetables checked in October 2026

The distance printed on an airport's website is the least useful number about it. What matters after landing is how long it takes, on public transport, to stand in the middle of the city holding a ticket that works on everything else. Geneva's trains reach Cornavin in six or seven minutes.67 A Ryanair arrival at Girona faces a coach of about 75 minutes before it reaches Barcelona at all.75

This index takes 30 airports serving European cities and asks each the same questions. How quickly does the fastest regular public transport reach a named central hub? Is it a train, metro or tram from the terminal, or does the trip start in traffic? How often does it go? Does anything run all night? And how far is the airport from that hub in a straight line? The answers come from airport operators, railways, transport authorities and city tourist offices, checked in October 2026, and the scoring rules are set out in full so you can argue with them.

Taxis, ride-hailing and hotel shuttles are left out on purpose. So are fares, which change too often to be worth printing in a piece meant to last; where a route charges a premium or a supplement, the notes say so.

Companion piece. For how hubs, transfers and alternative airports shape the wider journey, see The Airport Systems Atlas.

Distances were measured from each airport's reference point in the OurAirports dataset1 to the hub's location in OpenStreetMap.2

How the score works

Every airport is scored out of 100 on five measures. Time carries the most weight because it is what a traveller feels; the rest reward the things that make an arrival easy to trust.

Time to the hubup to 4040 points, minus half a point for every minute over five. Six minutes scores 39.5; 45 minutes scores 20; 85 minutes or more scores nothing.
One seat on rails20, 10 or 020 if a train, metro or tram runs from the airport to the hub with no change. 10 if rail reaches the airport but you have to change. Nothing if the trip begins on the road.
How oftenup to 15Departures on the fastest route, or any route scheduled within ten minutes of it: every 10 minutes or better scores 15; up to every 15 minutes, 10; up to every 30, 5. Departures timed to flights, or an interval we could not verify, score nothing.
Late at night15, 8 or 015 if public transport runs to the centre all night, every night. 8 if the last departures are around midnight or later, or the service meets every flight. Nothing if it stops earlier or we could not verify it.
Proximity10, 5 or 0Straight-line distance from airport to hub: up to 15 km scores 10; up to 30 km, 5; further, nothing.

Where a source gives a range of journey times, the score uses the lower figure and the table shows the range. A field we could not confirm scores zero and is marked as not verified.

The ten easiest arrivals

  1. =1

    Frankfurt

    96.5/100

    Frankfurt (Main) Hbf in about 12 min, 11.6 km away as the crow flies

    The S-Bahn leaves from beneath Terminal 1 and reaches the Hauptbahnhof in about 12 minutes, roughly every ten minutes by day.11 The S8 keeps running through the night, hourly or half-hourly.12 The only trap is the airport's second station: the Long-Distance Station is for ICE trains, and the S-Bahn goes from the Regional Station.10

  2. =1

    Copenhagen

    96.5/100

    København H in 12–15 min, 8.4 km away as the crow flies

    Trains from Terminal 3 reach Copenhagen Central in 12 to 15 minutes,2023 and the driverless M2 metro from the same terminal runs through the night, every 15 to 20 minutes, to Kongens Nytorv and Nørreport.2122 The airport is 8.4 km from the central station in a straight line, close enough to cycle.

  3. 3

    Amsterdam Schiphol

    95.5/100

    Amsterdam Centraal in 14–17 min, 12.1 km away as the crow flies

    Schiphol's station sits directly under the main arrivals plaza. About eight trains an hour run to Amsterdam Centraal in 14 to 17 minutes, and the airport says trains to Amsterdam run around the clock.8 After about 01:00 the service drops to one train an hour.9

  4. 4

    Zurich

    91/100

    Zürich HB in 9–13 min, 8.9 km away as the crow flies

    Around 13 trains an hour leave the station beneath the Airport Centre for the city, and every southbound train stops at Zürich HB, nine to 13 minutes away.3 There is no premium express and no need for one. Zurich loses most of its points because weekday nights have no all-night trains; the ZVV night network runs on Friday and Saturday nights.5

  5. 5

    Barcelona El Prat

    89.5/100

    Passeig de Gràcia in about 26 min, 12.8 km away as the crow flies

    The Rodalies R2 Nord reaches Passeig de Gràcia in about 26 minutes, but it runs only every half hour and only from Terminal 2.707374 What lifts Barcelona is the Aerobús, which runs every five to ten minutes to Plaça Catalunya in about 35, and a proper night network of Aerobús and NitBus services.70

  6. =6

    Geneva

    87.5/100

    Genève-Cornavin in 6–7 min, 4.2 km away as the crow flies

    The fastest link in the index: trains from the station under the terminal reach Cornavin in six or seven minutes, and the airport is 4.2 km from it in a straight line.67 Geneva loses ground on frequency (about five trains an hour) and on late nights, when trains stop around midnight.7

  7. =6

    Edinburgh

    87.5/100

    Princes Street / Waverley in 30–35 min, 11.3 km away as the crow flies

    Trams leave from just outside the terminal every seven minutes by day and reach Princes Street in about half an hour.58 The Airlink 100 express bus covers the same ground in about the same time and runs 24 hours, which gives Edinburgh full marks for late arrivals.6061

  8. 8

    Brussels

    86.5/100

    Bruxelles-Central in about 18 min, 10.9 km away as the crow flies

    The station is directly beneath the terminal halls, with six direct trains an hour to central Brussels. Bruxelles-Central is about 18 minutes away and Bruxelles-Nord about 11.50 Trains stop around midnight.

  9. 9

    Lisbon

    85.5/100

    Saldanha in about 20 min, 5.2 km away as the crow flies

    Lisbon's airport is barely five kilometres from Saldanha in a straight line, and the Red Line metro gets there in about 20 minutes, every six to eight minutes.7880 The metro closes around 01:00. Terminal 2, used by low-cost airlines, has no metro station, so its passengers start with a shuttle bus.79

  10. 10

    London Heathrow

    85/100

    London Paddington in 15 min, 20.2 km away as the crow flies

    Heathrow Express reaches Paddington in 15 minutes from the Terminal 2 and 3 station, every 15 minutes.35 Paddington is on the western edge of central London, and the Elizabeth line covers the same run in about 28 minutes at ordinary fares.3638 The N9 night bus runs all night, so a midnight arrival still has a way in.38

Distance is a poor guide

Oslo Gardermoen is 36.9 km from Oslo S in a straight line, and Arlanda is 36.1 km from Stockholm Central. Dedicated airport lines get you there in 19 and 18 minutes.2428 Munich is closer, at 29.1 km, but its S-Bahn is a suburban railway that stops all the way in, and the trip takes about 40 minutes.13

Dublin is the starkest case the other way. Its airport is 8.6 km from O'Connell Street, nearer than Schiphol is to Amsterdam Centraal or Frankfurt Airport is to the Hauptbahnhof. With no rail link, though, every option shares the road, and the coach takes anything from 20 to 40 minutes depending on traffic.55 Prague and Budapest are in the same position. Prague's quickest route is a trolleybus to the metro at Nádraží Veleslavín and Line A into the centre, about half an hour with one change; a rail branch is in preparation and Railway Pro reports full completion of the wider project expected in 2030.9699100 Budapest's 100E express bus takes about 40 minutes and runs around the clock; in March 2026 a government official said a rail link could be in use by 2031.101103

The premium expresses tell their own story. Heathrow Express, Flytoget, Arlanda Express and the Leonardo Express all charge more than the ordinary route. At Heathrow and Oslo the ordinary trains lose little: the Elizabeth line takes about 28 minutes to Paddington and Vy's regional trains about 23 to Oslo S.3627 At Arlanda the SL commuter train takes about 38 minutes and leaves from a separate station that charges its own fee.28 In Rome the cheaper FL1 regional train skips Termini altogether.84

Most often missed. Barcelona's R2 Nord train leaves only from Terminal 2.71 Lisbon's metro serves only Terminal 1.79 Frankfurt and Charles de Gaulle each have two stations, and the wrong one costs time.1045

Airports named for somewhere else

Plenty of low-cost flights land a long way from the city printed on the ticket. Four of them sit at the bottom of this index, and the reason is the road between the runway and the city.

Paris Beauvais is 65 km from Porte Maillot in a straight line and, by the regional tourist board's reckoning, about 85 km from Paris. Its shuttle coach takes 75 to 90 minutes, leaving about 20 minutes after each flight lands; going the other way it leaves Porte Maillot about three hours before each departure.484649

Brussels South Charleroi is 42.5 km from Bruxelles-Midi. The Flibco coach takes 55 to 60 minutes and runs to a fixed timetable, every 20 minutes by Flibco's account and every 30 by the airport's. The alternative is the TEC A1 bus to Charleroi-Central, the station called Charleroi-Sud until December 2022, and a train from there.515254

Girona, sold as Barcelona, is about 90 km from the city centre with no rail link. The Sagalés coach to Barcelona Nord takes about 75 minutes and is timed to Ryanair's flights.757677

Milan Bergamo is 44 km from Milano Centrale, about 50 to 55 minutes by coach.90 A rail link of about 5 km to Bergamo station is under construction. FS Italiane's project page gives activation in 2028; in February 2026 RFI said the works had fallen behind, and in June 2026 Il Giorno reported a delay of more than a year and a completion target of December 2027.939495

London Stansted is further out than any of them except Beauvais and Girona, 46 km from Liverpool Street, yet scores far better because a train leaves from beneath the terminal every 15 minutes and reaches Liverpool Street in 48 to 50 minutes.4243

Before you rely on it

Timetables change, and engineering works change them more often. Three cases stood out in October 2026. In Vienna the S-Bahn trunk line is closed from September 2026 until the end of October 2027, and the City Airport Train runs as a coach to Wien Mitte, taking about 21 minutes, until its trains return; ÖBB's Railjets to the Hauptbahnhof, measured here, are unaffected.171819 At Gatwick, the only overnight Gatwick Express trains in summer 2026 ran on Saturday and Monday mornings between July and September.41 In Rome, coaches to Termini leave through the night after the last Leonardo Express.8382

Madrid scores nothing for frequency because no source we could read published the Line 8 metro interval; esMadrid says Metro trains generally run every two minutes at the morning peak and every 15 after midnight.67 Bergamo's late-night coach times were not published on the pages we could check. Check the operator's own timetable before a late or early flight.
