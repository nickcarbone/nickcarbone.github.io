A directory of twenty rooms

The Hotel Bar Atlas

Twenty hotel bars around the world where anyone can walk in, take a seat and watch a city, sorted by the kind of evening they offer.

Nick Carbone · Field guide

A good hotel bar is one of the few grand rooms in a city that anyone can use for the price of a drink. Plenty of the people at the Long Bar in Singapore or the Connaught in Mayfair are not staying upstairs, and nobody asks. The rooms were built to impress travellers, which also makes them good places to watch a city at its habits: the after-work round in Stockholm, aperitivo in a Roman courtyard, a veteran jazz band on the Bund.

This atlas collects twenty of them in eighteen cities, sorted by the kind of evening they give you. Each entry has a register: how to get in, whether there is a cover charge, what to wear, whether there is a counter to sit at alone, what a drink costs and when to go. Policies and prices were checked against the bars' own pages on 19 October 2026 wherever they publish them; where only an older or secondary figure exists, the entry says so and dates it. All of it changes, so check before you go.

House rules for the non-resident

  1. Arrive near opening

    Several of the busiest bars here take no bookings at all, among them the Connaught, the Long Bar and the New York Bar in Tokyo. The advice from the Ritz itself, from Resy on Bemelmans and from The Nudge on the Savoy comes to the same thing: get there early.90,100,28,97,4,85

  2. Watch for the cover

    Music sometimes brings a charge. At Bemelmans it starts at 5.30pm and at the New York Bar at 6.30pm, so the hour before is the cheapest of the night.5,28

  3. Take the counter

    Alone, the stools are the best seats in the house: closest to the bartenders and, at Bemelmans, cheaper once the cover applies.4,5

  4. Dress a notch up

    Most of these bars publish a code, usually smart casual, and sportswear is the thing most often named as unwelcome.5,82,28,114

Painted rooms

1. Bemelmans Bar

The Carlyle · New York

Dates from 1947

Ludwig Bemelmans, the author of the Madeline books, painted the walls of this Upper East Side bar with Central Park through the four seasons, schoolgirls in two straight lines included. The bar opened in 1947, and the murals are the only Bemelmans commission that survives and is open to the public.1,2 He is said to have been paid in about 18 months' lodging for his family; Punch reports it as fact, while the hotel's own press kit calls it a rumour.3,1

The ceiling is 24-carat gold leaf, the bar black granite, and the piano sits in the middle of the room.1,3 The Carlyle opened in 1930 a block from Central Park, among the Madison Avenue galleries.2

Getting in
Walk-ins are still the main way in, with waits of up to two hours at peak; the few bookings carry a minimum spend.4
Cover
From 5.30pm, when the music starts: $20–25 a head at the bar, more at tables.5
Dress
“Dress for the occasion”; sportswear is discouraged.5

Bar stools, which are also the cheaper seats once the cover applies.4,5
Price

When
A weekday afternoon, before the pianist arrives.4

2. King Cole Bar

The St. Regis · New York

Dates from 1904 (hotel)

John Jacob Astor IV commissioned Maxfield Parrish's Old King Cole for his Knickerbocker Hotel at 42nd Street and Broadway, where it went up in 1906; Astor's own face is said to be the king's.7,8 The mural moved to Astor's St. Regis on Fifth Avenue in 1932, after years in storage and a spell in a Chicago museum, and was later restored at a cost of $100,000.7 Champalimaud Design reworked the room in 2024 with dark panelling and a green ceiling.9

The bar's claim to fame is the Red Snapper, the St. Regis name for the Bloody Mary, credited to the bartender Fernand Petiot in 1934. Petiot is also credited with making an early version at Harry's New York Bar in Paris, so “perfected here” is the safer claim.8,10 There are only about ten seats at the bar itself.8

3. Coq d'Or

The Drake · Chicago

Dates from 1933

The Coq d'Or opened on 6 December 1933, the day legal sales resumed in Illinois after Repeal, and the hotel says it held the second liquor licence issued in Chicago afterwards; the National Trust repeats the claim.12,13 It is a low-ceilinged room of dark panelling, red leather banquettes and French country murals, brightened in a 2011 restoration.12,14

The Drake opened in 1920 where Michigan Avenue meets the lake.12 Order the Executive Pour, a martini served with a chilled sidecar holding a second helping.15

4. Bar Longhi

The Gritti Palace · Venice

Dates from 1895 (hotel)

The Gritti was the residence of Doge Andrea Gritti from 1525 and became a hotel in 1895.18 Its bar is named after the 18th-century Venetian painter Pietro Longhi, and Cigar Aficionado reported that original Longhi paintings hang there, a detail other sources do not confirm.19 Expect etched Venetian mirrors, red marble floors and Louis XIV-style chairs.20,21

Outside, the terrace sits on the Grand Canal facing Santa Maria della Salute, styled after the wooden decks of 1930s Italian yachts.19,20 Hemingway stayed at the Gritti in the late 1940s and wrote parts of Across the River and into the Trees there; the story of a late-night baseball game in the lobby is best treated as legend.19,22

5. New York Bar

Park Hyatt Tokyo · Tokyo

Dates from 1994

On the 52nd floor of Kenzo Tange's Shinjuku Park Tower, the bar opened with the hotel in 1994 and reached a wider audience through Sofia Coppola's Lost in Translation.26 After a 19-month renovation by the Paris studio Jouin Manku, the hotel reopened on 9 December 2025 with the bar's black and chrome restored and its Valerio Adami murals in place.26,27

The hotel is known for its views over Tokyo and, on clear days, to Mount Fuji.26 Jazz sets start at 7.15pm. The L.I.T. cocktail, a nod to the film, is on the menu.28,29

6. Cadier Bar

Grand Hôtel · Stockholm

Dates from 2006

The Grand Hôtel opened in 1874, founded by Régis Cadier, a French chef who had cooked for the Swedish court.30 The bar named after him faces the harbour and the Royal Palace across the water.31,32 Difford's describes a marble counter about 16 metres long under a high plaster ceiling, with floor-to-ceiling windows.32

The hotel dates the present bar to 2006 and refreshed it in 2017; an earlier “Cadierbaren” was created in a 1985 renovation.33,31 Falstaff's 2026 bar guide put it first in Sweden.34 It opens at breakfast time and the hotel pitches it at Stockholmers as much as guests.35,36

7. La Terraza

Gran Hotel Ciudad de México · Mexico City

Dates from 1899 (building)

The building opened in 1899 as the Centro Mercantil, a department store, and became a hotel for the 1968 Olympics.37,38 Its stained-glass ceiling, completed in 1908, is often called Tiffany; it is the work of the French Art Nouveau designer Jacques Grüber.38,39 The wrought-iron cage lifts still run.40,39

Drinks are taken on the rooftop terrace, which looks over the Zócalo to the Palacio Nacional and the Metropolitan Cathedral.41,42 Oyster reports a lobby piano bar on some evenings, under the glass.43 You need not be staying to see the building.40

8. Harbour Bar

The Taj Mahal Palace · Mumbai

Dates from 1933

The Taj Mahal Palace opened on 16 December 1903 at Apollo Bunder in Colaba.45,46 The Harbour Bar dates itself to 1933 and calls itself Mumbai's first licensed bar.47,46 The claim's scope wanders, though: some write-ups make it the first licensed hotel bar, others the first licensed bar in India, and no licensing record has been published.48,49

Curved windows look over the harbour.48,47 The house story says two Americans sailing the Indian Ocean heard that Prohibition had ended, docked in Bombay and asked for a drink to celebrate; it is a good story with no documentation behind it.49

9. Stravinskij Bar

Hotel de Russie · Rome

Dates from 2000 (hotel reopened)

The hotel sits on Via del Babuino between Piazza del Popolo and the Spanish Steps, and the bar spills into a courtyard below terraced gardens laid out by Giuseppe Valadier and restored in 2020.50 Rocco Forte reopened the building as a hotel in 2000; the outdoor bar was redesigned by Paolo Moschino in 2024 and is heated in winter.50,51

Picasso and Cocteau stayed here in 1917 while working on Parade for the Ballets Russes.52,51 The bar takes Stravinsky's name, though no source found says he stayed.50 The hotel's own fact sheet bills it as an aperitivo address.53

10. Planet Bar

Mount Nelson · Cape Town

Dates from 2003

The Mount Nelson opened on 6 March 1899 for first-class passengers of the Union-Castle line, and has been pink since 1918, when its manager painted it to mark the end of the First World War.56 Planet Bar opened in October 2003 with a terrace over the gardens.56 Cape Town Magazine describes a solar-system model hanging from the ceiling.57

In January 2025 the hotel added The Fountain by Planet Bar, a garden offshoot that welcomes walk-ins.58,59 The hotel faces the Company's Garden, with Table Mountain behind.56

11. Bamboo Bar

Sofitel Legend Metropole · Hanoi

Dates from c. 2012

The Metropole opened in 1901 in the French Quarter, a short walk from Hoan Kiem Lake and the Opera House.61 Its poolside Bamboo Bar has a thatched roof, ceiling fans and wooden floors.62,63 It is a recent addition: in November 2011, piling work for the planned bar uncovered the hotel's wartime air-raid shelter.64

The singer Joan Baez sheltered there during the US bombing of Hanoi in December 1972.64,61 Tours of the shelter are for hotel guests only.65 The cocktails nod to past guests such as Graham Greene; Charlie Chaplin stayed in 1936.63,66

12. Carousel Bar

Hotel Monteleone · New Orleans

Dates from 1949

Twenty-five seats on a merry-go-round, painted with circus animals, make a full turn every 15 minutes. The bar opened in 1949 in the French Quarter hotel the Monteleone family has run since 1886, on Royal Street a block from Bourbon Street.69,70,71,72

The hotel's best-known drink, the Vieux Carré, was created in the 1930s by its head bartender, Walter Bergeron, and first printed in 1937. That is more than a decade before the Carousel turned, so “invented at the Carousel Bar” is loose.73,71 Live music plays seven nights a week.74

13. The Jazz Bar

Fairmont Peace Hotel · Shanghai

Dates from 1929 (hotel)

Victor Sassoon's Cathay Hotel opened on the Bund in 1929, an Art Deco tower with a green copper pyramid roof, and reopened as the Fairmont Peace Hotel in 2010.76 Noël Coward drafted Private Lives while staying there.76

The Old Jazz Band has played here since 1980. In 2017 the South China Morning Post counted six members aged 63 to 97 and noted the band had once been recognised by Guinness as the world's oldest; the line-up today is not documented.77,76 The room, once an English-style pub, is all dark wood, stools and stone pillars.77,78

14. American Bar

The Savoy · London

Dates from 1893 (hotel's date)

Richard D'Oyly Carte built the Savoy in 1889 on profits from the Gilbert and Sullivan operas at his theatre next door.80,81 The hotel dates its bar to 1893; the earliest record the Oxford Companion to Spirits and Cocktails cites is from 1903, the year Ada Coleman joined the bar.82,81

Coleman stayed until 1925 and is credited with the Hanky Panky. Harry Craddock succeeded her, and his Savoy Cocktail Book appeared in 1930.81 The room is grey and silver Art Deco, with a pianist every night.83,80

15. Connaught Bar

The Connaught · London

Dates from 2008

David Collins Studio designed the bar, which opened in August 2008 in the hotel's old American Bar: oak panelling finished in platinum silver leaf, linen panels in pink, pistachio and lilac, and leather with brogue detailing borrowed from Savile Row.86,87 It topped the World's 50 Best Bars list for 2020.88

The martini is mixed at your table from a trolley, with a choice of bitters.86 Outside, on the Mayfair corner of Carlos Place and Mount Street, Tadao Ando's water feature, Silence, has stood outside the entrance since 2011.89

16. Bar Hemingway

Ritz Paris · Paris

Dates from 1994 (named)

A wood-panelled room of 25 seats hung with Hemingway photographs and letters, on Place Vendôme.92,93 The bar took his name in 1994. Colin Field ran it from then until 2023 and created the Serendipity, made with Calvados, mint, apple juice and Champagne; Anne-Sophie Prestail succeeded him.94,93,92

The legend says Hemingway “liberated” the Ritz on 25 August 1944 and ran up a tab for 51 dry martinis. He did reach Paris that day as a war correspondent, but the Germans had already left the hotel, and the martini count is folklore.95,96

17. Long Bar

Raffles · Singapore

Dates from 1900s

Raffles opened in 1887 under the Armenian Sarkies brothers, and its main building was finished in 1899.99 The Long Bar began as tables in the hotel's Cad's Alley wing; today it occupies two floors of the Raffles Arcade, and guests still brush peanut shells onto the floor.100,101 The hotel reopened in 2019 after a restoration led by Alexandra Champalimaud.102

Raffles credits the Singapore Sling to its bartender Ngiam Tong Boon in 1915. Difford's points out that a pink sling appeared in Singapore newspapers in 1903 and that 1915 may be the year Ngiam died; treat the date as disputed.100,103

18. Orient Bar

Pera Palace · Istanbul

Dates from 1892–95 (hotel)

Built between 1892 and 1895 for passengers off the Orient Express, the Pera Palace was designed by Alexander Vallaury for the Wagons-Lits company.105,106 It reopened in 2010 after a restoration, with the Orient Bar back as a meeting place.107 Expect a long polished wooden bar, tall columns and chandeliers.108

The hotel and the Associated Press both say Hemingway drank here in the early 1920s, when he was covering the Greco-Turkish war.109,105 Agatha Christie's link to Room 411 belongs to hotel lore.105

19. Le Churchill

La Mamounia · Marrakech

Dates from 1923 (hotel conceived)

La Mamounia was conceived in 1923 by Morocco's railway company, on the edge of the medina facing the Koutoubia.112,113 Its bar is designed as a Pullman carriage, a nod to that railway past, in smoked oak with a black marble counter set with green light blocks by Patrick Jouin.114

Churchill wintered and painted at the hotel, and the bar is named for him.114,112,113 The menu prints his quotations beside the cocktails.114,113

20. Blaue Bar

Hotel Sacher · Vienna

Dates from 1876 (hotel)

Eduard Sacher, son of the man who invented the Sachertorte, opened the hotel in 1876 behind the State Opera; his widow Anna ran it after 1892, with her cigars and bulldogs.116,117 The bar is royal blue throughout: damask walls, crystal chandeliers, velvet seats and a gold-framed mirrored bar.118

In February 1948 Graham Greene heard about Vienna's sewers and its penicillin racket from a young British intelligence officer at the Sacher, details that went into The Third Man. Sources disagree on whether that conversation happened in the bar or over lunch.117,119
