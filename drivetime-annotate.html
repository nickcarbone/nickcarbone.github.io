How to map a drive (about 20 minutes per video)
  1. Pick a city and drive. Play it at 2× and watch for places you can find on the map: junctions, landmarks, bridges.
  2. When you know where the car is, press Space to pause and click that spot on the map. The pin records the video time.
  3. Pin every turn, a few car-lengths after the car has turned (pins in the middle of a junction can snap to the cross street). On long straight runs, add one about every minute.
  4. Drag a pin to move it. Click its time in the list to jump the video there.
  5. Press Snap to roads. Check the blue line follows the video. Any row flagged orange or red is worth a second look.
  6. Press Copy for page and paste the result inside const ROUTES = { … } in drivetime-tesla.html, with a comma between entries.

Work saves in this browser as you go. ←/→ seek 5s, Shift for 1s, Z removes the last pin.
